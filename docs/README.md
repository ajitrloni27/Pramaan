# PRAMAAN

**The Team Document · HackVerse Track 3 · 24 July 2026**

*One engine. Proof, not opinions. Built once, branches forever.*

---

## 1. The moment this starts with

Picture yourself at a hospital counter. You've been up all night with a family member. The clerk slides you a bill — four pages, two hundred lines. “Consumables ₹12,400. Procedure ₹45,000. Room ₹8,000 a day.” You don't know what any of it should cost. You're tired, you're scared, and you just pay it.

Now picture signing a rental agreement. “Standard format, just sign.” You sign. You don't notice the line that says your deposit is “non-refundable” — because you didn't know the law says it has to be refundable.

Now picture a delivery rider finishing a 10-hour shift, paid ₹487, with no way to check if that's right because the math is buried in forty lines he can't read.

**One thing is true in all three moments**: there is a paper in your hand, there is an official rule somewhere, and **nobody is comparing them for you at the one moment it matters**. You lose money, or a right, or a fair wage — not because you did anything wrong, but because the comparison is too tedious, too technical, or too fast for a human to do in the moment.

That's the whole problem. Everything we build is the answer to that one sentence.

## 2. The basis — two truths the whole thing stands on

**Truth 1 — It's always the same shape.** Whether it's a bill, a lease, a payslip, or a medicine strip, the problem is identical: *your paper* vs *the official rule*, and a gap between them. We're not solving six problems. We're solving **one** problem that shows up in six places. One trunk, many branches.

**Truth 2 — Don't guess. Prove.** An AI that says “I think you were overcharged” is dangerous — it can be wrong, and a wrong guess about your money or your rights is worse than no guess. So our AI **never gives an opinion**. It only ever shows you *your number*, *the official number*, *the gap*, and *where both numbers came from* — so you (or a judge, or a pharmacist) can check it yourself. **It produces proof, not opinions.**

If you remember nothing else, remember these two: **same shape everywhere; prove, don't guess.**

## 3. What it is

Pramaan is **one machine** that does one job: it takes a paper you've been handed, compares it to the official rule, shows you the gap as proof, and gets a letter ready — then **waits for you to tap** before it sends anything.

It is **not** six different apps. It's **one app** you can feed six different papers. Think of a weighing scale: one scale, and you can put apples, gold, or rice on it — the scale doesn't change, only what you weigh. Pramaan is the scale. The hospital bill, the lease, the payslip — those are just different things you put on it.

Because the machine is built once, **every new kind of paper we add later is cheap** — we teach it a new rule-book, we don't rebuild anything. New branches grow from the same trunk. That's why this is a *platform* and not just a *project*.

## 4. What it does — five steps, same every time

1. **Read** the paper — pull the numbers and words off it (photo or PDF).
2. **Look up** the official rule for that exact thing — from a source it can cite.
3. **Compare** — subtract your number from the official number; find the gap.
4. **Prove** — show the gap as a card: *“you: ₹45,000 → official: ₹18,000 → gap: ₹27,000”* — with a link back to where both numbers came from.
5. **Wait** — get a letter ready, then **stop and wait for your tap** before sending. It never acts on your behalf without you pressing the button, and it logs every button you pressed.

**On screen, one page, three boxes:**
- **Left:** your paper, with little rectangles around the parts it read.
- **Middle:** a stack of cards — *your number → official number → the gap* — turning red or green.
- **Right:** the ready letter, one button *“send (only after you tap),”* and a log of what you tapped.

That page is Pramaan. For a bill the cards show money; for a lease they show clauses. **The page never changes — only what's on it.**

## 5. Why this idea

We looked at many ideas — MSME cash-flow, disaster response, crop disease, more. Most were “AI predicts something and tells you.” We chose this for four reasons:

1. **It acts, it doesn't just advise.** It gets the letter ready and, on your tap, sends it.
2. **It's grounded, not fragile.** A cash-flow forecast on 20 invoices is a guess that will be confidently wrong. Comparing a bill to a published price list is **math, not a guess** — it can't hallucinate.
3. **It's on the citizen's side.** Every existing tool checks if *you're* following the rules. Almost none checks if *they're* following the rules that protect *you*. The big companies can't copy this without angering their own paying customers.
4. **It's a tree, not a single branch.** One engine, many papers — the difference between a project and a company.

## 6. The value it creates

**For the person using it:**
- Stop losing money and rights you didn't know you had.
- Get **proof you can hold**, not advice you must trust.
- Act in **seconds, at the counter**, not weeks later.
- Never exposed to a wrong guess — the machine stays quiet unless it can cite a source.

**For us as a team:**
- Build **one engine**; it pays back every time we add a paper type.
- Build something **startup-shaped**, not a throwaway demo.
- The hard, trust-earning part (proof, safety, log) is built **once** and shows up on every branch for free.

**For the judges:**
- A clean, demonstrable, defensible idea that scores on every criterion — and demos *reliably*, which beats a flashy idea that breaks on stage.

## 8. The honest part — what it is NOT

- **It does not give legal or medical advice.** It locates the rule and shows the gap. The judgment stays with a human. We say this out loud, every time.
- **It does not act on its own.** Nothing is sent, filed, or blocked without your tap; every tap is logged. This is the trust feature we sell, not a limitation we hide.
- **New branches are not free.** The *engine* is free to reuse; the *rule-book* per paper is still about a day of human work. We never say “infinite domains for free.” We say “the engine is free; each domain is a day of curation.”
- **It stays quiet when unsure.** If it can't read a line or find a source, it says *“unverified — check with a person,”* never a guess. Silence over a false alarm, every time.

## 9. The one line everyone memorizes

> **“Pramaan reads any paper you're handed, compares it to the official rule, and hands you the proof of the gap — then waits for your tap before it does anything.”**

Stage version:
> “It doesn't tell you what it thinks. It shows you the rule, the gap, and the proof — and only acts when you say so.”

The name is the pitch: **Pramaan** means *valid proof / verifiable evidence*. The name *is* the promise.

## 10. What we actually build

- **The trunk (build once):** read → compare → prove → wait-for-tap.
- **The grafting point (design once):** the three forms a new paper fills — *what to read*, *where the official answer lives*, *how to say the gap out loud*.
- **Branch 1 — fully grown (what the judge watches):** the **hospital bill**, with a planted overcharge it catches live.
- **Branch 2 — grafted rough (what earns the name):** the **rental agreement**, on the *same screen*, just enough to prove “one machine, many papers.”
- **Everything else:** mentioned, not built.

The rule: build **one** branch to perfection with the proof-and-tap mechanics, wire a **second** as a stub, **mock** every external send. One deep branch with a visible proof engine beats six shallow ones.

### For the builders — story words to tech words

| Story word | Tech word |
| :--- | :--- |
| read the paper | OCR + field extraction, with confidence score + on-page position |
| the official rule | versioned rule-book (lookup table; every row carries its official citation) |
| compare / the gap | deterministic code — subtraction + rule-matching, **not** the language model |
| the proof card | structured record: your value + official value + gap + confidence + two source links |
| prove, don't guess | LLM used **only** for plain-language summary + letter draft; both labeled “AI-generated, review before sending” |
| wait for your tap | consent gate: consequential actions execute only on explicit user authorization |
| the log of taps | immutable audit trail (the governance layer) |
| the trunk / the tree | one orchestration pipeline; each domain is a config (the three forms) |
| plug in a rule-book | MCP tool-connection standard for rule sources + outbound channels |
