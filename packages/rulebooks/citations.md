# PRAMAAN — CITATION ONE-PAGER
## The 6 Bulletproof Stats That Prove the Filter Exists

### 1. THE HOSPITAL FILTER (The Demo Wedge)
*   **Stat:** ~44% Out-of-Pocket Health Expenditure in India.
*   **Source:** National Health Accounts (NHA) Estimates 2019-20, WHO.
*   **Meaning:** Nearly half of all health spending comes directly from patients' pockets at the point of care — the exact pool where overcharging happens because no one audits the line items.
*   **Pramaan Anchor:** CGHS Rate Card 2024 (Sch II #214) vs. Hospital Bill Line 47.

### 2. THE MEDICINE TRAP (The Subtle Kill)
*   **Stat:** Hospitals routinely charge 5–10x MRP for consumables (gloves, syringes, paracetamol).
*   **Source:** Legal Metrology Act, 2009 + DPCO 2013 + NPPA Price List (Jan 2026).
*   **Meaning:** It is illegal to charge above MRP, but enforcement is near-zero because no patient checks 200 line items at 3 a.m.
*   **Pramaan Anchor:** NPPA Notification S.O. 1897(E) (Paracetamol 650mg = ₹2.15) vs. Bill Line 89 (₹45).

### 3. THE RENTAL FILTER (The Tree Proof)
*   **Stat:** Housing/Real Estate is a Top-3 Consumer Grievance Category in India.
*   **Source:** India Justice Report 2026 / National Consumer Commission Data.
*   **Meaning:** Tenants sign illegal clauses (non-refundable deposits, lock-ins) because they need a house *now*, and fighting it takes decades.
*   **Pramaan Anchor:** Model Tenancy Act 2021, Section 11(2) (Refundable Deposit) vs. Lease Clause 1.

### 4. THE GIG FILTER (The Scale)
*   **Stat:** ~40% of gig workers earn under ₹15,000/month after costs.
*   **Source:** Economic Survey 2025-26.
*   **Meaning:** Platforms bury the math. A rider gets paid ₹487 for a 10-hour shift. The breakdown is 40 lines of "base pay," "distance pay," "surge," "deductions." No human can reverse-engineer that in real-time.
*   **Pramaan Anchor:** Motor Vehicle Aggregator Guidelines 2025, Clause 17 (Driver Share ≥ 80%) vs. Payslip Line 12.

### 5. THE SAFETY FILTER (The CDSCO Hook)
*   **Stat:** ~150–240 "Not of Standard Quality" (NSQ) drug samples flagged by CDSCO every month.
*   **Source:** CDSCO Monthly Alerts (June 2025).
*   **Meaning:** The regulator flags hundreds of bad batches monthly. This data is public, but it's a PDF list. No patient at a pharmacy counter checks the batch number against the CDSCO list before swallowing the pill.
*   **Pramaan Anchor:** CDSCO NSQ List June 2025 (Batch AYC-2407 Azithromycin) vs. Medicine Strip Batch No.

### 6. THE INSURANCE FILTER (The Asymmetry)
*   **Stat:** ~11% of health insurance claims are rejected; 1.37 Lakh complaints filed in FY25 (+41%).
*   **Source:** IRDAI Annual Report 2024-25 / Bima Bharosa Portal.
*   **Meaning:** Insurers cite "Policy Clause 7.3." The patient doesn't know if Clause 7.3 actually applies to their surgery. They accept the rejection because fighting an insurance company feels impossible.
*   **Pramaan Anchor:** IRDAI Master Circular on Health Insurance 2024 (Moratorium Period 60 months) vs. Rejection Letter Date.
